03 · Investigation

Examine the information.

The purpose is not to accuse people. It is to examine information, show what is right or wrong through evidence, and make a better understanding possible.

We can investigate claims found in videos, images, text, and websites. We begin by understanding exactly what is being claimed, then look for reliable sources and compare the claim with the evidence.

We should explain what the evidence supports, what it contradicts, and what remains uncertain. We must be willing to check our own understanding, too.

03 · Investigation

Four simple steps.

1 · Identify

Write down the claim and find its original context.

2 · Examine

Check sources, evidence, and other reliable information.

3 · Explain

Separate established facts, interpretations, and unknowns.

4 · Correct

Share fair findings so people can understand and respond.

04 · What comes next

Truth should lead somewhere.

When someone recognizes that an action is wrong, the next step should not be only to point at the mistake. It should be possible to understand the truth, correct the action, and repair the harm where possible.

04 · What comes next

Understand, correct, repair, help.

1 · Understand

Look at the evidence. Understand what happened, what is known, and what remains uncertain.

2 · Correct

Recognize the choice and take a practical step toward doing what is right.

3 · Repair

Where harm has been caused, look for a responsible way to make things right.

4 · Help

Make useful knowledge and practical help accessible to people who need them.

05 · My work

My work, on many platforms.

My work does not live in only one place. I run it across several website platforms, each with its own purpose, so truth can reach people wherever they already look for answers.

On these platforms I share my testimony, my investigations, and clear explanations. Every piece starts with a claim, is checked against reliable sources, and ends by showing what the evidence supports, what it contradicts, and what remains uncertain.

The platforms also work together. Something examined in one place can be explained in another, and the people who read it can question it, respond to it, and help correct it. The goal is the same everywhere: make truth known, and make practical help easier to reach for those who need it.
